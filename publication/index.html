My research uses single-cell analysis to study immune-cell states in infection, inflammation, and metabolic disease. An asterisk (*) marks equal first-author contribution.

Original Research

Single-cell heterogeneity of adipose eosinophils is shaped by sex and RELMα and linked to macrophage communication networks in obesity

Rebecca E. Ruggiero-Ruff* and Xinru Qiu* et al. · PNAS Nexus · 2026 · Co-first author

Single-cell analysis identified eosinophil states that differ by sex, helminth infection, and RELMα status. I performed the single-cell RNA-seq analysis; flow cytometry and tissue assays provided additional evidence for eosinophil heterogeneity.
